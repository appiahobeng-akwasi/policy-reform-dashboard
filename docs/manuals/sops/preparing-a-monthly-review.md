# SOP: Preparing a monthly review

Who does it: the Technical Secretariat (a programme administrator). Takes 30 minutes, plus chasing.

## A week before the review

1. Check the reminders are going out: **Jobs** shows the daily job *Finished* each day.
2. Open **Executive** and **Pathway**. Note the reforms with many stale or overdue items.
3. Ask owners to update their items or confirm nothing changed. For owners without an account, use the follow-up list in **Notices** and enter their updates under **For someone else**.

## Two working days before (the cut-off)

1. Approve or return everything waiting under **Approvals**, so the pack shows approved data.
2. Check that the month's indicator values are in (they are due by the 5th).
3. Open **Reviews**, choose *Monthly Pillar 1 review* and the review date, and select **Prepare the pack**.
4. Read it through:
   - **Prepare:** does each reform's rating and stage look right? Sample data is labelled; it must not be presented as real.
   - **Review:** the escalations at levels 2 and 3 are the agenda. Stalled stages are listed with them.
   - **Decide:** the open decisions, their tier and follow-ups.
   - **Track:** what was implemented, and follow-up actions still open.
5. If late updates arrive, approve them and prepare the pack again. The new version replaces nothing: both are kept.

## Sending it

1. Select **Download the file** (in English, then switch to FR and download again for a French copy), or **Print or save as PDF**.
2. Send it to the review participants with the agenda. The file opens offline in any browser.

## At and after the meeting

1. Note each decision in the blank rows of **Decide**: what was decided, the tier, the follow-up, the owner and the due date.
2. Within two working days, enter the decisions and follow-up actions through **Bulk upload** (Decisions and Actions sheets). Ask a second administrator to approve them.
3. The next pack shows them under **Track**.
